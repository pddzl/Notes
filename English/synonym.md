
## 混乱

These three words all describe **disorder**, but they focus on **different kinds of disorder**.

|Word|Meaning|Degree|Focus|Common Examples|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**mess**|Untidy or dirty state|⭐|Untidiness|room, desk, hair|
|**jumble**|Things mixed together without order|⭐⭐|Mixed arrangement|clothes, ideas, words|
|**chaos**|Complete disorder; no control|⭐⭐⭐|Situation or system|traffic, war, office|

Think of them as a progression:

> **mess → jumble → chaos**

- **mess** = untidy
- **jumble** = mixed up
- **chaos** = completely out of control

### 1. Mess

A **mess** is something that is **untidy, dirty, or disorganized**.

It often refers to a place or someone's appearance.

```text
My room is a mess.
Your desk is a mess.
His hair is a mess.
The kitchen is a mess after dinner.
```

Typical collocations:

```text
make a mess
clean up a mess
a messy room
```


> **Focus:** It needs cleaning or organizing.

### 2. Jumble

A **jumble** is a **mixed-up collection of things**.

The emphasis is not on dirt, but on things being **mixed together randomly**.

Examples

```text
a jumble of clothes
a jumble of books
a jumble of ideas
a jumble of wires
```

Example:

> My suitcase is a **jumble** of clothes.

The clothes aren't necessarily dirty—they're just mixed together.

> **Focus:** Mixed together.

### 3. Chaos

**Chaos** means **complete disorder** where people or systems have lost control.

It usually describes **events or situations**, not physical objects.

Examples

```text
The airport was in chaos after the storm.
Traffic was chaos this morning.
The classroom descended into chaos.
The company is in chaos.
```

Common collocations:

```text
total chaos
utter chaos
political chaos
economic chaos
```

> **Focus:** Nobody is in control.

## 伤害

### 1. detrimental

普通用词，客观 “会造成伤害”，可用于人、物、化学物质
### 2. harmful

**偏抽象、间接损害**，多指对利益、发展、机会、效率产生不良影响，不多指肉体直接受伤；语气更强，正式书面。

## 法律

### law
单部法律，可数，生效执行；可以指科学定律
### legislation
立法行为 / 整套法规，不可数，不用 a，无复数；多指议会审议中的法案

## 增强

### strength

**v.**（最通用）加强；变强

> 核心：使力量、实力、关系、制度变强，可以由弱变强，也可以由一般变得更强；适用范围最广

- strengthen the economy 壮大经济
- strengthen ties 加强关系
- strengthen security 加强安保
- strengthen muscles 增强肌肉力量

> 语感：力量、实力、韧性得到提升

### reinforce

**v.** 加固；强化（观点、信念）；加深印象

> 核心：**把原有东西加固，防止坏掉 / 动摇；让已有想法变得更牢固** 1. 实物：加固建筑、结构 reinforce the wall 加固墙体 2. 抽象：强化观点、刻板印象、信念、记忆（本来就存在，进一步巩固）

- reinforce existing prejudices 加深固有的偏见
- reinforce an argument 佐证 / 强化论点

> 语感：本来就有，**加固、补强，防止被破坏**

### enhance

**v.** 提高，增强；美化

> 核心：**提升品质、价值、体验、吸引力，让变得更好、更出彩**，不侧重 “牢固”，侧重 “品质升级”

- enhance efficiency 提升效率
- enhance one’s reputation 提高声誉
- enhance the flavour 提升风味
- enhance user experience 改善用户体验

> 语感：锦上添花，**让质量 / 体验 / 价值变得更优**

## 污染

## 连续

### consecutive

核心：连续的、依次相接，强调【一个接一个，有序、完整计数】，事物彼此独立，前后挨着，中间有空隙也没关系，多用于数字、天数、场次

- consecutive days 连续数日
- three consecutive wins 三连胜
- 5 consecutive numbers 五个连续数字

> It rained for three consecutive days. 连续三天下雨（一天一天依次算起，可以理解为每天独立，统计上相连）
### successive

核心：相继发生、接踵而至；侧重一件事跟着另一件发生，侧重先后次序与更迭，书面感更强；很多场景可和 consecutive 互换，但侧重点不同

✅ successive 更强调**更替、一波接一波**；
✅ consecutive 侧重**按顺序计数、连成序列**

- successive governments 一届接一届的政府（政权更迭，首选 successive，不用 consecutive）
- successive failures 接连不断的失败

> Successive waves of immigrants arrived. 一波又一波移民接踵而至。

consecutive vs successive 最简区分

- 单纯罗列数字、次数、赛程计数 → **consecutive**
- 事物更迭、一代接一代、一波接一波 → **successive**
### continuous

核心：不间断、无中断（时间 / 空间上连成一片）
名词：continuity

- continuous rain 连绵不断的雨（雨一直下，没有停）
- continuous noise 持续不断的噪音
⚠️ 区别 continual：continual 是反复发生、中间有停顿

> The machine runs continuously. 机器不间断运转。

### sequential

核心：按既定逻辑顺序、有先后步骤，强调【逻辑次序】，不一定紧密相连，重点是不能乱序；技术、程序术语高频

- sequential steps 循序步骤
- sequential data 序列数据（计算机）
- sequential order 先后次序

> These tasks must be completed in sequential order. 这些任务必须按先后顺序完成。

## 保留

一句话区分口诀

✅ retain = 保住（不丢）
✅ maintain = 维持（不变、保养）
✅ sustain = 支撑（续命 / 承受冲击）

### retain

核心：保有、留住，防止失去；侧重 “所有权、占有、记忆、人员”
强调东西本来属于自己，努力保住，不流失。

搭配： 
retain rights 保留权利
retain staff/customers 留住员工 / 客户
retain information 记住信息
retain moisture 锁住水分

例句： The company retained ownership of the land. 这家公司保留土地所有权。

### maintain

核心：维持、保养；使现状持续不变；不恶化
最常用：状态、设备、关系、水平、秩序

搭配：
maintain order 维持秩序
maintain speed 保持速度
maintain equipment 保养设备
maintain a relationship
维系关系 maintain silence
保持沉默

额外独有含义：坚称，主张
He maintains he is innocent. 他坚称自己无辜。

### sustain

核心：支撑、承受；维持生命 / 长期运转；扛住冲击
两层高频场景：

① 供给能量，维持生命运转
sustain life 维持生命
sustain growth 维持增长
② 承受（伤害、损失、压力）
sustain damage 遭受损坏
sustain losses 蒙受损失
