## 什么是副词

副词（Adverb）是一类用来修饰动词、形容词、其他副词，甚至整个句子**的词。

副词主要表示：

- 时间（When）
    
- 地点（Where）
    
- 方式（How）
    
- 程度（How much）
    
- 频率（How often）
    
- 语气/评价（Sentence Adverb）
    

可以记住一句话：

> **副词 = 给动作、状态或整个句子添加"怎么、什么时候、在哪里、多么、多久一次"等说明。**

---

## 副词修饰什么

副词可以修饰四种对象。

### 1. 修饰动词（最常见）

说明动作是怎样发生的。

```text
He runs quickly.
```

```
runs
 ↑
quickly
```

更多例子：

```text
She speaks slowly.
They worked hard.
The baby slept soundly.
```

---

### 2. 修饰形容词

表示程度。

```text
She is very beautiful.
```

```
beautiful
    ↑
  very
```

更多例子：

```text
extremely cold
quite good
really interesting
```

---

### 3. 修饰其他副词

表示程度。

```text
He runs very quickly.
```

```
runs
 ↑
quickly
 ↑
very
```

更多例子：

```text
He speaks extremely slowly.
She answered quite confidently.
```

---

### 4. 修饰整个句子

表示说话人的评价或态度。

```text
Fortunately, nobody was hurt.
```

这里 Fortunately 修饰整个句子，而不是某一个词。

常见句子副词：

- Fortunately
    
- Luckily
    
- Obviously
    
- Honestly
    
- Surprisingly
    
- Sadly
    

---

## 副词分类

### 1. 方式副词（Adverbs of Manner）

#### 用法

修饰动词，描述动作是**如何发生的**。

#### 常见形式

很多以 **-ly** 结尾。

```
quick → quickly
slow → slowly
careful → carefully
happy → happily
```

但不是所有方式副词都有 **-ly**。

例如：

```
fast
hard
well
```

#### 位置

通常放在：

- 动词后
    
- 句尾
    

#### 例子

```text
He runs quickly.
The teacher explained the concept clearly.
The baby slept soundly.
She sang beautifully.
```

---

### 2. 频率副词（Adverbs of Frequency）

#### 用法

表示动作发生的频率。

#### 常见单词

```
always
usually
often
sometimes
rarely
seldom
never
```

#### 位置

一般放在：

- 实义动词之前

```text
I often read books.
```

- be 动词之后

```text
She is always happy.
```

- 助动词之后

```text
He has already finished.
```

#### 例子

```text
She always visits the gallery on weekends.
My family usually has dinner at 7 P.M.
I sometimes read a book before bed.
The athlete rarely eats fast food.
```

---

### 3. 程度副词（Adverbs of Degree）

#### 用法

表示程度、强弱。

可以修饰：

- 动词
    
- 形容词
    
- 副词
    
#### 常见单词

```
very
really
quite
fairly
too
almost
nearly
extremely
completely
```

#### 位置

通常放在被修饰词之前。

#### 例子

修饰形容词：

```text
She's very beautiful.
He's fairly happy.
```

修饰副词：

```text
He runs very quickly.
She speaks extremely slowly.
```

修饰动词：

```text
She nearly finished her work.
I completely forgot.
```

---

### 4. 时间副词（Adverbs of Time）

#### 用法

表示动作发生的时间。

#### 常见单词

```
now
today
yesterday
tomorrow
soon
already
recently
```

#### 位置

通常放在句尾，也可以放句首。

#### 例子

```text
I came yesterday.
We'll leave tomorrow.
She has already finished.
```

---

### 5. 地点副词（Adverbs of Place）

#### 用法

表示动作发生的位置。

#### 常见单词

```
here
there
inside
outside
upstairs
downstairs
everywhere
```

#### 例子

```text
Come here.
Please wait outside.
Children were playing upstairs.
```

---

### 6. 句子副词（Sentence Adverbs）

#### 用法

修饰整个句子，表达说话人的态度、评价或判断。

#### 常见单词

```
Fortunately
Luckily
Obviously
Honestly
Sadly
Surprisingly
```

#### 例子

```text
Fortunately, nobody was hurt.
Honestly, I don't know.
Obviously, he was wrong.
```

---

## 副词的位置总结

|修饰对象|常见位置|示例|
|---|---|---|
|动词|动词后、句尾|He runs quickly.|
|形容词|形容词前|very beautiful|
|副词|副词前|very quickly|
|整个句子|句首|Fortunately, ...|
|频率副词|实义动词前；be 动词后；助动词后|I often read. / She is always happy.|

## 比较级 & 最高级

副词与形容词一样，也有比较级和最高级。

||比较级|最高级|
|---|---|---|
|含义|更……|最……|
|句式|A + 动词 + 比较级 + than + B|主语 + 动词 + the + 最高级 (+ 比较范围)|
|构成|-er / more + 副词|-est / the most + 副词|
|不规则|well → better|well → best|

例如：

```text
Tom runs faster than Mike.
```

```text
She speaks more clearly than her classmates.
```

```text
Among all the students, she speaks the most fluently.
```

## 如何快速判断是不是副词

可以问下面几个问题：

| 问题                                      | 常见副词                  |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| How?（怎样）                                | quickly, carefully    |
| When?（什么时候）                             | yesterday, now        |
| Where?（在哪里）                             | here, outside         |
| How often?（多久一次）                        | always, often         |
| How much?（到什么程度）                        | very, almost          |
| What is the speaker's attitude?（说话人的态度） | fortunately, honestly |

如果一个词是在回答这些问题，那么它大概率就是副词。

## 形容词 vs 副词

|形容词（Adjective）|副词（Adverb）|
|---|---|
|修饰**名词**|修饰**动词、形容词、副词、整个句子**|
|a beautiful girl|sings beautifully|
|a careful driver|drives carefully|
|a fast car|runs fast|

**一句话总结：**

- **形容词**：描述**人或事物是什么样的**。
    
- **副词**：描述**动作如何发生、程度如何、什么时候、在哪里，以及说话人的态度**。
    

> **记忆口诀：**
> 
> **形容词修名词，副词修四样：动词、形容词、副词、整个句子。**
