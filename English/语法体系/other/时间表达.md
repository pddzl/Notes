## 什么时候使用时间介词

英语中常用 at, on, in 表示时间。

基本规则：

| 介词  | 核心含义    | 常见时间                                     |
| --- | ------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| at  | 具体时间点   | at 8:00、at noon、at midnight              |
| on  | 具体某一天   | on Monday、on July 1st、on Christmas Day   |
| in  | 较长的时间范围 | in July、in 2026、in winter、in the morning |

## at 具体时间点

### 用法

用于表示比较具体的时间点：

- 时刻
- 正午
- 午夜
- 日出、日落等

### 常见搭配

```text
at 8:00
at noon
at midnight
at sunrise
at sunset
```

### 例句

> I'll meet you **at 8:00**.

我八点和你见面。

## on 具体某一天

### 用法

用于表示：

- 星期
- 具体日期
- 具体某一天的节日

### 常见搭配

```text
on Monday
on Friday
on July 1st
on Christmas Day
on New Year's Eve
```

### 例句

> We celebrate on New Year's Eve.

我们在除夕庆祝

## in 较长的时间范围

### 用法

用于表示：

- 月份
- 年份
- 季节
- 世纪
- 一天中的时间段

### 常见搭配

```text
in July
in 2026
in winter
in the morning
in the afternoon
in the evening
```

### 例句

> She was born **in 1998**.

她出生于 1998 年。

## 时间限定词

有些时间表达本身已经包含了时间范围或频率信息，因此通常**不再使用 at / on / in**。

常见的时间限定词：

- every
- each
- this
- that
- next
- last

### every

```text
every day
every Monday
every month
every year
every New Year's Eve

I exercise every day.
We meet every Friday.
They have a family gathering every New Year's Eve.
```

不能说：

> ❌ in every day

> ❌ in every New Year's Eve

### this / that

```text
this morning
this week
this month
this year

that morning
that day
that year

We met this morning.
I'll finish it this week.
```

### next / last

```text
next Monday
next week
next month
next year

last Friday
last week
last month
last year
```

```text
I'll see you next Monday.
She arrived last night.
He graduated last year.
```

不能说：

> ❌ on next Monday

> ❌ in next week

> ❌ on last night

## 为什么不能说 in every New Year's Eve

例如：

```text
every New Year's Eve
```

结构：

```text
every
  ↓
New Year's Eve
```

`every` 已经限定了 `New Year's Eve`，整个短语已经可以直接表示时间。

因此：

> They have a family gathering every New Year's Eve.

而不是：

> ❌ They have a family gathering in every New Year's Eve.

## on every Monday 是不是错误？

不是绝对错误，但日常英语中通常直接使用：

> We meet every Monday.

而：

> We meet on every Monday.

更强调“每一个星期一”中的每一个具体星期一，使用场景相对特殊。

因此日常表达优先记：

> every Monday

## 不要把规则理解成“没有限定词就一定要加介词”

这是一个重要的注意点。

有些时间词本身就可以直接作时间状语：

```text
today
tomorrow
yesterday
tonight
```

例如：

```text
I'll see you tomorrow.
She arrived yesterday.
I'm staying home tonight.
```
