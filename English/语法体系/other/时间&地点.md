## 地点

表示某事发生在某地
### 位置

+ 句尾
+ 句首加逗号

## 时间

表示某事发生在某个时间

### 位置

+ 句尾
+ 句首加逗号
+ 和地点连用时, 地点在前时间在后

`They have a family gathering every New Year's Eve.`
`Every New Year's Eve, they have a family gathering.`
`They have a family gathering at grandma's house every NewYear's Eve.`
`Every New Year's Eve, they have a family gathering at grandma's house.`

## 后置修饰

修饰/限定某人/某物
### 位置

在被修饰/限定的人/物后面

`People from Sichuan like to eat spicy food`
`The architecture in the downtown area reflects the city's historical development`
`The party last night was a lot of fun`
