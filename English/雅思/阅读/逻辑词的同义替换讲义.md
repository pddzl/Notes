## 并列

### and / or / as well as / along with / together with

  

（和；并 / 或者 / 既...又；都 / 和...一起 / 和...一起）

  

**例句**：The editors **and/as well as/along with/together with** the illustrators are working overtime. 编辑和插画师都在加班工作。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅17-Test 4-Passage 1**

- 题干：... prevent the spread of disease by eating ____ **and** blackflies. 通过吃 ____ 和黑蝇来防止疾病传播。

- 原文：... feed not just on crop pests but also on mosquitoes... **as well as** blackflies, which spread river blindness. ...不仅以农作物害虫为食，还以蚊子...以及黑蝇（传播河盲症）为食。

- 答案：mosquitoes

  

### not only... but also... / both...and / either... or

  

（不仅...还 / 二者都 / 二者之一）

  

**例句**：

  

- **Not only** the editors **but also** the illustrators are working overtime. / **Both** the editors **and** the illustrators are working overtime. 编辑和插画师都在加班工作。

- **Either** the editors **or** the illustrators are working overtime. 编辑或是插画师在加班工作。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅17-Test 4-Passage 3**

- 题干：**In addition**, the scans raised the possibility of unusual strength in the parts of his brain that deal with ____ input. 此外，扫描结果显示，他大脑中处理 ____ 输入的部分可能存在异常强度。

- 原文：The scans **also** suggest that Gareyev's visual network is more highly connected to other brain parts than usual. 扫描结果还显示，Gareyev的视觉网络与大脑其他部分的连接强度异于常人。

- 答案：visual

- **剑雅17-Test 3-Passage 1**

- 题干：reduction in ____ **and** available sources of food were partly responsible for decline in Tasmania. Tasmania的衰退部分是由于 ____ 和可用食物来源的减少所造成的。

- 原文：These include competition with wild dogs introduced by European settlers, loss of habitat **along with** the disappearance of prey species, and a distemper-like disease which may also have affected the thylacine. 这些包括与欧洲移民引入的野狗竞争，栖息地的丧失以及猎物的消失，以及类似犬瘟热这样影响到袋狼的疾病。

- 答案：habitat

  

### also / too / similarly / at the same time / in addition

  

（也 / 也 / 类似地；也 / 同时 / 也；除此之外）

  

**例句**：

  

- The editors are working overtime. **Also/Similarly/At the same time/In addition/Apart from that**, the illustrators are working extra hours.

- The editors are working overtime. The illustrators are working extra hours **too**.

  

编辑和插画师都在加班工作。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅15-Test 2-Passage 1**

- 题干：**Another** result is that some people cross the road in a ____ way. 另一个结果是，有些人会以一种____方式穿越马路。

- 原文：In doing so they make crossings feel longer, introducing psychological barriers greatly impacting those that are the least mobile, **and** encouraging others to make dangerous crossings to get around the guard rails. 他们这样做会让人们觉得过路时间更长，引入心理障碍，对那些行动不便的人造成极大的影响，并鼓励其他人绕过护栏进行危险的穿越。

- 答案：dangerous

  

### another / additional

  

（另一个 / 额外的）

  

**例句**：The editors are working overtime. **Another/An additional** group of people that are working extra hours are the illustrators. 编辑和插画师都在加班工作。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅17-Test 4-Passage 2**

- 题干：Cases like this illustrate how the guilds could prevent ____ **and** stop skilled people from working. 像这样的案例说明协会如何防止 ____ 和阻止技术人才工作。

- 原文：The dominance of guilds **not only** prevented people from using their skills, **but also** held back even the simplest industrial innovation. 行会的主导地位不仅阻止了人们发挥自己的技能，而且还阻碍了最简单的工业创新。

- 答案：innovation

  

## 因果

  

### due to / because of / as a result of

  

（由于 / 因为 / 由于）

  

**例句**：**Due to/Because of/As a result of** the need to cut cost, the company cut down on staff. 这家公司为了减少开支而裁员。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅15-Test 1-Passage 2**

- 题干：... most motor accidents are partly **due to** ____. ...大多数交通事故的部分原因是 ____。

- 原文：... more than 90 percent of road collisions involve human error **as a contributory factor**. 90%以上的道路碰撞事故都与人为失误有关。

- 答案：human error

  

### cause / explain / account for / be the reason for / be a contributory factor for / come with

  

（造成 / 解释 / 解释 / 是...的原因 / 是造成...的原因 / 使...随之而来）

  

**例句**：The company's need to cut cost **causes/results in/explains/is the reason for/is a contributory factor for/accounts for** its cutting down on staff. 这家公司为了减少开支而裁员。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅17-Test 2-Passage 2**

- 题干：An undesirable trait such as loss of ____ may **be caused by** a mutation in a tomato gene. 不良的性状，例如失去 ____ 可能是由于番茄基因突变引起的。

- 原文：And sometimes the desirable mutations **come with** less desirable traits. For instance, the tomato strains grown for supermarkets have lost much of their flavour. 有时，有利的基因突变也随着不太理想的特征。例如，为了适应超市销售，番茄品种失去了很多口味。

- 答案：flavour

  

### therefore / consequently / as a result / ultimately / eventually / finally / at last / in the end

  

（所以 / 因此，结果 / 结果 / 结果 / 结果 / 结果 / 结果 / 结果）

  

**例句**：This company had to cut costs. **Consequently/As a result/Therefore/Ultimately/Eventually/Finally/At last/In the end**, it cut down on staff. 这家公司为了减少开支而裁员。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅17-Test 3-Passage 3**

- 题干：... **explain why** skyscrapers are absent from ____. ...解释为什么____没有摩天大楼。

- 原文：... **is the reason for** skyscrapers not being built between the two urban centers. ...是两个城市中心之间未建造摩天大楼的原因。

- 答案：specific areas（对应 two urban centers）

  

## 目的

  

### to / in order to / for the purpose of

  

（为了... / 为了... / 为了...）

  

**例句**：This company had cut down on staff **to cut/in order to cut/for the purpose of cutting** costs. 这家公司为了减少开支而裁员。

  

**真题示例**

  

- **剑雅15-Test 2-Passage 2**

- 题干：According to Church, introducing Asian elephants to the tundra would involve certain physical adaptations **to** minimise ____. Church认为，将亚洲象引入冻原需要进行某些身体适应性调整，以尽量减少 ____。

- 原文：Necessary adaptations would include smaller ears, thicker hair, and extra insulating fat, **all for the purpose of** reducing heat loss in the tundra. 必要的适应方式包括耳朵变小、毛发变厚和增加隔热脂肪，所有这些都是为了减少冻原上的热量损失。

- 答案：heat loss

  

## 举例

  

### such as / like / for instance/example / say

  

（比如 / 比如，就像 / 比如 / 比如）

  

**例句**：

  

- Large electrical goods, **like/such as** television sets and washing machines, are not included in this deal.

- Large electrical goods, **say,/for instance,/for example,** television sets and washing machines, are not included in this deal.

  

本次交易不涉及诸如电视机和洗衣机之类的大型电器。

- **剑雅15-Test 1-Passage 3**

- 题干：He believes there is no need for further exploration of Earth's ____, except to answer specific questions **such as** how buffalo eat. 他认为，除了回答具体问题（比如水牛如何进食）外，没有必要进一步探索地球____。

- 原文：We know how the land surface of our planet lies; exploration of it is now down to the details - the habits of microbes, **say**, or the grazing behaviour of buffalo. 我们知道地球的陆地表面是如何形成的；对它的探索现在只剩下细节——比如说微生物的习性，或者水牛的吃草行为。

- 答案：surface
