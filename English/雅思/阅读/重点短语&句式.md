## 38. so that/such that

`so that`
`so as to`
以便，为了

`so ... that ...`
`such ... that ...`
如此...以至于...

```text
She practiced hard so that she could perform well at the concert.  
She practiced hard so as to perform well at the concert.  
她刻苦练习，以便能在音乐会上表现得很好  

The book was so interesting that I couldn't put it down.  
The book was such an interesting read that I couldn't put it down.  
这本书如此有趣，以至于我放不下
```

```text
她生气地走**出**了房间

打印机墨水用完了

He did it out of respect for his parents.  
他这样做是出于对父母的尊重。
```
