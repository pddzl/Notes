### The area you live in

#### Do you like the area that you live in

我的思路: 喜欢，因为生活便利，环境优美，邻居也很友好。

Yes, I really like my neighborhood because it is very convenient for daily life. The streets are clean and full of green trees, which makes the area beautiful. Also, my neighbors are quite friendly and we often greet each other warmly.
