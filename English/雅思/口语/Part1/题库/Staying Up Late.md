## Q1. Do you often stay up late?

`Not really. I usually go to bed before midnight because I need to get up early for work. But sometimes I stay up late using my phone at weekends.`

## Q2. Why do people stay up late?

`I think there are several reasons. Some people have a lot of work to do, while others stay up late to watch videos or play games. Personally, I think using smartphones before bed is one of the main reasons.`

## Q3. Did you stay up late when you were a child?

`No, not very often. My parents always asked me to go to bed early because they believed getting enough sleep was important. So, i usually went to bed before nine o'clock.`

## Q4. What do you usually do when you stay up late?

`I usually use my phone to listen to music or watch videos. But most of the time, I just scroll on my phone aimlessly. I try not to stay up too late because I feel tired the next day.`

## Q5. Do you think staying up late is bad for your health?

`Yes, definitely. Staying up late can make people feel tired and affect their concentration the next day. I think getting enough sleep is important for both physical and mental health.`

## Q6. Do you prefer to stay up late or get up early?

`I prefer to get up early because I feel more energetic in the morning. I can get more things done before work. Besides, going to bed early helps me maintain a healthy routine.`

## 建议你这样记忆，不要逐字背诵

你之前提到过，逐篇背诵完整的雅思口语答案比较困难。因此，这组题目也可以采用**关键词记忆法**。

先记住下面这条回答逻辑：

**直接回答 → 原因 → 具体情况或结果**

例如，回答第一个问题：

**Do you often stay up late?**

记忆关键词：

`Not really → before midnight → get up early for work → sometimes too much work`

看到关键词，就尝试自己组织句子：

1. Not really.
2. I usually go to bed before midnight.
3. I need to get up early for work.
4. But sometimes I stay up late when I have too much work to do.

这样，你记住的是表达思路，而不是整段文字。

## 一个特别实用的技巧

这六个问题其实可以共用一套素材：

- **习惯：** I usually go to bed before midnight.
- **原因：** I need to get up early for work.
- **活动：** I sometimes use my computer to study English.
- **影响：** I feel tired the next day.
- **观点：** I think getting enough sleep is important.
- **偏好：** I prefer to get up early.

你只需要熟悉这些表达，就能应对多个相关问题。

**练习建议：** 先不要急着背六篇答案。每天选择两个问题，看着英文问题，用关键词尝试回答两三遍。熟悉之后，再尝试不看参考答案独立表达。
