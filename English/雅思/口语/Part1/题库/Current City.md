## Q1. Where are you living now?

你现在住在哪里？

Sample Answer

I'm currently living in Wuxi, which is also in Jiangsu Province. It's a fairly developed city with good public transportation and plenty of shopping facilities. I've been living here for a while, and I find it quite convenient.

记忆词：

- currently living in Wuxi
    
- Jiangsu Province
    
- fairly developed
    
- public transportation
    
- shopping facilities
    
- quite convenient
