## Q1. Describe a party you enjoyed

### Background

`I'm going to talk about a party I enjoyed recently. A friend of mine held a small gathering at his house to celebrate his birthday, inviting about five or six close friends including me. The atmosphere was really warm and relaxed since we all knew each other well. We spent the evening chatting, laughing, and enjoying some homemade food together without any pressure. It was a simple event, but everyone had a wonderful time celebrating with him in such a comfortable setting.`

### Main Description

`The best part was how natural everything felt because nobody was trying to act formal or stiff. We divided tasks like preparing food, decorating the room, and playing music, which made us feel like a team. Since we were all good friends, there was never an awkward silence or any uncomfortable moments during the whole night. This freedom made it the most memorable and enjoyable party I have attended this year by far.`

### Details and Feelings

`Two friends really stood out; one kept everyone laughing while another took many photos for us to remember the night. However, I felt a bit bored when one guest kept talking about funds and stocks, so I just nodded politely. Later, two best friends and I walked by the river, enjoying the breeze and talking about our lives until midnight. That quiet moment made the whole experience even more special and warm for me personally.`

- At his house
- 5–6 close friends
- Warm and relaxed
- Chatting, laughing, homemade food

**2. Main Description — Fun Activities**

- Work as a team
- No awkward silence
- Memorable and enjoyable

**3. Details & Feelings — Special Moments**

- Friend 1: funny jokes 😂

- Walk by the river 🌙
- Cool breeze, talk until midnight
- Special and warm
