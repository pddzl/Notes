## Q1. A friend I didn't like at first

### Background

I'd like to talk about a friend who I didn't like at first but became good friends with later.

I first met him when we were at secondary school, and we ended up sitting next to each other in class for two years. At that time, I thought he was a little arrogant, because he always looked confident, dressed really well and seemed to spend a lot of time on his appearance. To be honest, I assumed he was the kind of person who only cared about looking good, so I didn't really want to talk to him.

### Main Description

However, after we were put in the same study group, we started spending much more time together, and I gradually realized that I had been wrong about him. He was actually one of the friendliest and most helpful people I've ever met.

For example, whenever I struggled with my homework, he would patiently explain things to me until I fully understood, even if it took up his own free time. He also has a great sense of humor, so being around him made school life a lot less stressful. Little by little, we became close friends.

### Details and Feelings

Looking back, I'm really glad I gave him a chance, because he turned out to be completely different from my first impression. This experience taught me a valuable lesson: I shouldn't judge people by their appearance or by first impressions, because they can be totally misleading.

Now he's still one of my best friends, and I really appreciate our friendship. Every time I'm tempted to make a snap judgment about someone, I remind myself of him and of how wrong I was.

## Q2. A friend who is good at dressing up

### Background

I'd like to talk about one of my friends who dresses really well. He's actually the same friend I mentioned in the previous story — the one I didn't like at first because I thought he was too proud. Ironically, his sense of style was part of that wrong first impression, but now I see it as one of his best qualities.

### Main Description

One thing I really admire about him is his sense of style. He always wears clean, well-fitted and fashionable clothes, and he knows how to match different colors so that everything looks put together. What's impressive is that he doesn't rely on expensive brands — most of his clothes are quite affordable, but he somehow makes simple outfits look smart.

He also keeps up with the latest trends. He watches videos from fashion bloggers and reads articles about dressing well, so he always knows what's in style and what suits him.

### Details and Feelings

Whenever I need to go somewhere important, like a job interview or a formal dinner, I ask him for advice about what to wear. He's always happy to help, and his suggestions have saved me from some embarrassing outfit choices. Thanks to him, I feel much more confident about my appearance.

That's why I like him so much — he's not only good at dressing up, but also genuinely kind and generous with his advice. I'm glad I got to know the real person behind that first impression.

**3. Details & Feelings — Advice**
