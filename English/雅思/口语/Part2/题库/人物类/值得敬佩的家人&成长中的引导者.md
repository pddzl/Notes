## Q1. Describe someone who is older than you that you admire.

### Background

I'm going to talk about my older cousin, who is someone I really admire.

She is a few years older than me. When I was a child, she often helped me with my homework and taught me many useful things.

### Main Description

One thing I remember clearly is that she was always extremely hardworking when she was a student.

She spent a lot of time studying because she wanted to become a doctor. I know it wasn't easy for her, as she had to deal with a lot of pressure and study for many years.

But she never gave up, and eventually achieved her goal. Now she works as a doctor.

### Details & Feelings

Another thing I admire about her is her patience.

When I was young, she even taught me how to make a small wooden stool. She explained everything patiently and helped me whenever I made a mistake.

I think she is a great role model for me because she has taught me the importance of patience and hard work.

I'm really proud of her.

### 🧠 Memory Map

**1. Background — My Cousin**

- Older cousin, a few years older
- Helped with my homework as a child
- Kind & patient

**2. Main Description — Her Achievement**

- Hardworking student → dream: become a doctor
- Years of study + heavy pressure
- Never gave up → became a doctor

**3. Details & Feelings — Why I Admire Her**

- Taught me to make a wooden stool, patiently
- Patience + hard work → my role model
- Really proud of her

### 🔑 Remember the Story

Older cousin → helped with homework → hardworking student → became a doctor → wooden stool → patience → role model

## Q5. Describe a lesson that impressed you a lot.

### Background

I'm going to talk about a lesson I learned from my older cousin when I was a child.

She was a few years older than me, and she often helped me with my homework and taught me useful skills.

### Main Description

One lesson that impressed me was when she taught me how to make a small wooden stool.

At that time, I didn't have much experience making things by hand, so I found the process quite difficult.

She showed me how to measure and cut the wooden boards and how to put the different pieces together.

Whenever I made a mistake, she didn't get impatient. Instead, she explained things again and encouraged me to try one more time.

Eventually, we finished the stool together.

### Details & Feelings

Through this experience, I learned that patience and practice are very important when learning something new.

I also realized that making mistakes is a normal part of learning, and I shouldn't give up too easily.

This lesson has stayed with me for a long time.

I think my cousin was not only teaching me how to make a stool, but also teaching me how to deal with difficulties.

That's why this experience impressed me so much.

### 🧠 Memory Map

**1. Background — The Lesson**

- From my older cousin, as a child
- The lesson: making a wooden stool

**2. Main Description — The Process**

- No experience → found it difficult
- Measure, cut, assemble
- Mistakes → she never got impatient → explained again, encouraged me

**3. Details & Feelings — The Takeaway**

- Patience + practice matter when learning
- Mistakes are normal → don't give up easily
- Not just a stool → how to deal with difficulties
- Lesson stayed with me

### 🔑 Remember the Story

Wooden stool → difficult at first → mistakes → patient encouragement → finished together → patience & practice → dealing with difficulties
