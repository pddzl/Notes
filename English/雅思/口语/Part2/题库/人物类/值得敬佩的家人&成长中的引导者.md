## Q1. Describe someone who is older than you that you admire.

### Background

I'm going to talk about my older cousin, who is someone I really admire.

She is a few years older than me. When I was a child, she often helped me with my homework and taught me many useful things.

### Main Description

One thing I remember clearly is that she was always extremely hardworking when she was a student.

She spent a lot of time studying because she wanted to become a doctor. I know it wasn't easy for her, as she had to deal with a lot of pressure and study for many years.

But she never gave up, and eventually achieved her goal. Now she works as a doctor.

### Details & Feelings

Another thing I admire about her is her patience.

When I was young, she even taught me how to make a small wooden stool. She explained everything patiently and helped me whenever I made a mistake.

I think she is a great role model for me because she has taught me the importance of patience and hard work.

I'm really proud of her.

## Q2. Describe a person who likes to make things by hand.

### Background

I'm going to talk about my older cousin, who is quite good at making things by hand.

She is a few years older than me, and when I was a child, she often spent time doing practical activities with me.

### Main Description

One experience I remember clearly was when she taught me how to make a small wooden stool.

She prepared some wooden boards and simple tools, and showed me how to measure and cut the wood.

After that, she taught me how to put the different pieces together to make a stool.

At first, I found it quite difficult because I didn't have much experience, but she was very patient and guided me through the whole process.

### Details & Feelings

Eventually, we finished the stool together. Although it wasn't perfect, I felt really proud of what we had made.

I think making things by hand is a very interesting activity because you can turn simple materials into something useful.

I really enjoyed the experience, and it also helped me understand that making something requires patience and attention to detail.

That's why I remember this experience so clearly.

## Q3. Describe a new skill you learned when you were a child.

### Background

I'm going to talk about a practical skill I learned when I was a child.

It was making a small wooden stool, and my older cousin was the person who taught me.

### Main Description

I remember that I was quite young at that time, and I was curious about making things by hand.

My cousin prepared some wooden boards and simple tools. First, she showed me how to measure and cut the wood.

Then she taught me how to put the different pieces together to make a stool.

At first, I found it quite challenging because I wasn't familiar with the tools or the process.

But she explained everything patiently and helped me whenever I made a mistake.

Eventually, we managed to finish the stool together.

### Details & Feelings

I felt really excited and proud because it was the first time I had made something useful with my own hands.

Although it was just a simple wooden stool, the experience gave me a sense of achievement.

More importantly, I learned that if I keep practising and don't give up easily, I can learn new things.

It's still one of my most memorable childhood experiences.

## Q5. Describe a lesson that impressed you a lot.

### Background

I'm going to talk about a lesson I learned from my older cousin when I was a child.

She was a few years older than me, and she often helped me with my homework and taught me useful skills.

### Main Description

One lesson that impressed me was when she taught me how to make a small wooden stool.

At that time, I didn't have much experience making things by hand, so I found the process quite difficult.

She showed me how to measure and cut the wooden boards and how to put the different pieces together.

Whenever I made a mistake, she didn't get impatient. Instead, she explained things again and encouraged me to try one more time.

Eventually, we finished the stool together.

### Details & Feelings

Through this experience, I learned that patience and practice are very important when learning something new.

I also realized that making mistakes is a normal part of learning, and I shouldn't give up too easily.

This lesson has stayed with me for a long time.

I think my cousin was not only teaching me how to make a stool, but also teaching me how to deal with difficulties.

That's why this experience impressed me so much.
