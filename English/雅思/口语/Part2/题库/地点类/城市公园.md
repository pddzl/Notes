### 1. Describe a natural place in your city that you enjoy visiting

自然场所｜城市公园
#### Background

`I'm going to talk about a lovely park in my city that I really enjoy visiting. It's just across the river from my home, so it's very convenient to get there. I simply cross the main road and walk over a small bridge nearby. I usually go there at weekends when I have some free time, especially when I want to get away from my busy work schedule and enjoy some fresh air.`

#### Main description

`The park is located where two rivers meet. It has large green lawns, lots of trees and walking paths along the river. Many people come here to exercise in the evening, but my favorite thing to do is stand by the river and watch the boats go by. The peaceful view helps me clear my mind and forget about work for a while. I also enjoy taking a slow walk along the riverbank to blow off steam after a stressful week.`

#### Details and feelings

`There's also an old red building by the river that has been turned into a coffee shop. I love sitting by the window, drinking coffee, reading a book or listening to music. However, the park can get quite noisy and crowded on sunny weekends, when people dance to music, street vendors sell snacks and families have picnics on the grass. Once, I even fell asleep on a bench and lost my favorite headphones. I searched everywhere but couldn't find them, which made me really upset. Since then, I've been much more careful with my belongings.`

`Overall, I love this park because it's close to home and gives me a chance to relax and enjoy nature.`

### 2. Describe a building you enjoy visiting (e.g. a library, a shopping mall, etc.)

建筑物｜河边的红色咖啡馆
#### Background

`I'm going to talk about an old red building in a lovely park across the river from my home. I often visit this park at weekends, and this building is one of my favorite places to spend a relaxing afternoon. It's easy to get there because I just need to cross the main road and walk over a small bridge.`

#### Main description

`The building is right by the river and has traditional curved roofs, so it looks quite historic and has a lot of character. It used to serve a different purpose, but now it's been turned into a cozy coffee shop. What I like most about it is the large windows overlooking the river. The red walls look beautiful against the blue water, especially on a sunny day, and the view makes a great backdrop for photos.`

`Inside, the atmosphere is comfortable and relaxing. I usually order a cup of coffee, sit near the window and read a book or listen to music. Sometimes, I can spend a whole afternoon there without getting bored.`

#### Details and feelings

`The only downside is that the park gets noisy and crowded at weekends, especially when the weather is nice. There may be people dancing outdoors, street vendors selling snacks and families having picnics nearby. So, if I want to enjoy some peace and quiet, I usually try to arrive earlier in the day.`

`I really like this building because it combines traditional architecture with a modern use. For me, it's not just a coffee shop but also a perfect place to slow down and take a break from everyday life.`

### 3. Describe a noisy place you have been to

嘈杂的地方｜周末的城市公园
#### Background

`I'm going to talk about a noisy place I've been to, which is a park just across the river from my home. I usually go there at weekends to relax and enjoy the fresh air. It's normally a pleasant place, but it can get surprisingly noisy when lots of people gather there.`

#### Main description

`The noise is usually worst on weekend evenings. Some elderly people play traditional Chinese music on their erhus, while groups of people dance to music coming from loudspeakers. At the same time, street vendors shout to attract customers, and children run around and scream with excitement. All these sounds mix together, making it difficult to have a quiet conversation or simply enjoy the sound of nature.`

`Although the park is quite spacious, some areas near the river become packed with people. The loud music and constant chatter can be overwhelming, especially if you're looking for somewhere peaceful to unwind.`

#### Details and feelings

`I remember one time when I went there hoping to relax. I sat on a bench by the river, listened to music through my headphones and accidentally fell asleep. When I woke up, I realized my headphones were missing. I searched around the bench and along the path I'd taken, but I couldn't find them anywhere.`

`I felt really upset because I'd had those headphones for years. Since then, I've been more careful about my personal belongings.`

`Although the noise can be annoying, I still visit the park because it's close to home and offers a nice view of the river. I just try to avoid the busiest times now.`

### 4. Describe a crowded place you went to

拥挤的地方｜晴朗周末的公园
#### Background

`I'm going to talk about a crowded place I went to, which is a popular park just across the river from my home. I usually visit it at weekends because it's convenient to get to and offers lovely views of the river. However, it can become extremely crowded when the weather is good.`

#### Main description

`I remember going there one sunny Sunday afternoon. The weather was perfect for spending time outdoors, so lots of families came to the park for picnics and camping. Almost every patch of grass was covered with tents, picnic mats and people sitting around chatting. Children were running and shouting everywhere, while some groups were playing music or dancing.`

`The traffic was another big problem. There were so many cars trying to get into the parking lot that the line of vehicles moved extremely slowly. We ended up waiting for about half an hour just to get into the parking lot. It was really frustrating because we spent so much time waiting before we could even start enjoying our afternoon.`

#### Details and feelings

`Once we finally parked the car and walked into the park, it was still packed with people. It was difficult to find a quiet place to sit, and the noise from the music and street vendors made the atmosphere even more chaotic.`

`Although I like the park, I don't really enjoy visiting it when it's that crowded. These days, I prefer going early in the morning or choosing a quieter day. That way, I can enjoy the scenery without dealing with traffic jams and huge crowds.`

### 5. Describe an occasion when you lost something in a public place

丢东西的经历｜河边长椅上的耳机
#### Background

`I'm going to talk about a time when I lost something in a public place. It happened in a lovely park just across the river from my home. I often go there at weekends to relax, take a walk and enjoy the fresh air. Since it's so close to my home, it's one of the places I visit most often.`

#### Main description

`One sunny afternoon, I was sitting on a bench by the river, listening to music with my favorite headphones. The weather was warm, the breeze was gentle and the view was so peaceful that I gradually became sleepy. Before I knew it, I'd fallen asleep on the bench.`

`When I woke up, I realized that my headphones were gone. At first, I thought they might have fallen onto the ground, so I checked under the bench and searched the grass around me. I even walked back along the path I'd taken, hoping that someone might have picked them up and left them somewhere nearby. Unfortunately, I couldn't find them anywhere.`

#### Details and feelings

`I was really upset because I'd used those headphones for years, and they meant quite a lot to me. I also felt frustrated with myself because I hadn't taken proper care of my belongings.

`The park itself is a beautiful place, with large lawns, plenty of trees and two rivers meeting nearby. There's also an old red building by the river that has been turned into a coffee shop. I still enjoy visiting the park, but ever since that incident, I've been much more careful with my personal belongings. I always make sure to put them somewhere safe before I leave a place.`

`It was an unpleasant experience, but it taught me a simple lesson: even when you're relaxing, you should keep an eye on your valuables.`
