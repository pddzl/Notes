## Q1. Describe a time when you made an important decision and were happy with the result.

### Background

I'm going to talk about an important decision I made a few years ago. At that time, I was working in IT operations, mainly dealing with server issues and monitoring systems. Although the job was quite stable, I gradually got bored with doing the same maintenance tasks every day. I felt that I wasn't learning enough new things, so I decided to switch to software development.

### Main Description

It wasn't an easy decision because I had almost no programming experience. I knew I would have to start from scratch and spend a lot of time learning new skills. However, I was really interested in building things rather than just fixing existing problems.

So, I started learning programming after work and enrolled in an online coding course. I also spent a lot of time practicing by building small projects. Eventually, I managed to get a job as a junior software developer and started working on real projects.

### Details and Feelings

To make this happen, I saved money to pay for professional coding courses and buy a better computer. I also met a senior developer at a tech conference who gave me some useful advice about learning programming.

Looking back, I'm really happy with this decision because it completely changed my career path. I now have more opportunities to learn new things, and I feel much more satisfied with my work.

## Q2. Describe something you do regularly that you think is a waste of time.

### Main Description

`For example, I still spend too much time checking old system logs every morning. I used to do this because I needed to make sure all the servers were running properly and there were no unusual errors.`

`However, I've already moved to a new team, and other colleagues are now responsible for this task. So, checking those logs every day isn't really necessary anymore.`

`Sometimes I spend nearly an hour going through different log files, even though there's usually nothing important to report. I find it quite frustrating because I could use that time to learn new programming skills or work on my own projects.`

### Details and Feelings

`But now I realise that I need to manage my time better and focus on more important things. I'd rather spend that time improving my coding skills and becoming a better developer.`

## Q3. Describe a time when you saved money to buy something you wanted.

### Main Description

I wanted to enrol in an online coding bootcamp recommended by a senior developer I really admired. However, the course was quite expensive, so I decided to save money for it.

For about six months, I stopped eating out, ordered less takeaway food and tried to avoid buying unnecessary things. I saved almost half of my monthly salary whenever possible.

At first, it was quite difficult because I had to give up some things I enjoyed. But I kept reminding myself that the course could help me develop the skills I needed for my new career.

Finally, after six months, I had saved enough money and purchased the full course. I was really excited because I could finally start learning from professional instructors.

### Details and Feelings

I chose this course because it offered practical projects rather than just theoretical knowledge. I wanted to learn how to write real code and build useful applications.

Looking back, I think saving money for this course was definitely worth it. It gave me a good starting point for my career change and helped me become more confident about learning programming.

## Q4. Describe a time when you received good service from a staff member in a shop.

I'm going to talk about a time when I received excellent service from a staff member in an electronics store.

### Main Description

When I visited the store, I was quite confused because there were several MacBook models available, and I wasn't sure which one would be suitable for my needs.

A staff member noticed that I was having trouble making a decision, so he came over and offered to help.

He spent quite a long time explaining the differences between the models, including their performance, memory and battery life. He also asked me what I mainly planned to use the laptop for.

After listening to my needs, he recommended a MacBook Air instead of a more expensive model. He explained that it was powerful enough for learning programming and handling most of my daily development tasks.

I really appreciated that he didn't just try to sell me the most expensive product.

### Details and Feelings

What impressed me most was his patience and professional attitude. He explained everything in simple language, so even someone without much knowledge of computer hardware could understand.

In the end, I decided to buy the MacBook Air. I was really happy with the experience because I found a laptop that suited both my needs and my budget.

## 背诵建议

我建议你按照下面的顺序练习，而不是按照 Q1 到 Q6 的顺序机械背诵

1. 先熟悉 Q1：这是整个故事的主线，理解为什么从运维转行到开发
2. 接着练习 Q3 和 Q4：它们都是为了转行做准备，一个是购买课程，一个是购买 MacBook
3. 然后练习 Q5 和 Q6：两道题使用同一位技术创业者，分别强调采访经历和人物特点
4. 最后练习 Q2：它是一个独立的日常习惯故事，但背景仍然来自以前的运维工作

还有一个小技巧：

Memory Map 用中文理解故事，用英文关键词回忆内容，最后用自己的英语组织句子

不要追求每次说出来的句子都和范文一模一样。只要故事逻辑、关键词和重要细节没有丢失，就算成功

这样，你实际上只需要掌握一条职业转型主线，加上 5 个辅助情节，就可以应对这 6 道 Part 2 题目了
