Part2 引出话题的句型

描述人、地点、物品、媒体时，用在Part 2开头的句子

- ﻿﻿The xx that I'm going to tell you about (describe/talk about) is...  
    我要告诉（描述/谈论）你的xx是……
- ﻿﻿I'm going to tell you about (describe/talk about)...  
    我要告诉你（描述/谈论）••••

描述事件、经历时，用在Part 2开头的句子

- ﻿﻿The time that I'm going to tell you about (describe/talk about) is when I did sth.  
    我将要告诉你（描述/谈论）的时间是我做某事的时候。
- ﻿﻿I'm going to tell you about (describe/talk about) the time when I did sth.  
    我要告诉你（描述/谈论）我做某事的时间。

用于引出/切换话题的句型

- ﻿﻿As for...  
    就⋯•⋯•方面说
- ﻿﻿In terms of...  
    就••⋯・而言
- ﻿﻿Speaking of/about...  
    说到⋯•⋯•
- ﻿﻿When it comes to...  
    说到•
- ﻿﻿As far as... is concerned...  
    就…・⋯・而言
- ﻿﻿Moving on to...  
    说到⋯••••

例句：

- ﻿﻿The movie that I'm going to talk about is a thrilling suspenseful ride that will keep you on the edge of your seat.
- ﻿﻿As far as technology is concerned, smartphones have revolutionized the way we communicate and access information in today's fast-paced world.
