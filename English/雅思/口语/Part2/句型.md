# 引出话题

## 一、描述人、地点、物品、媒体

用于 Part 2 开头，引出要描述的对象。

### 1. The ... that I'm going to talk about is ...

**含义：** 我要谈论的……是……

**结构：**

The + 名词 + that I'm going to talk about is + 描述对象

**例句：**

The movie that I'm going to talk about is a thrilling, suspenseful ride that will keep you on the edge of your seat.

我要谈论的这部电影是一段惊险刺激、扣人心弦的体验，会让你全程屏息以待。

### 2. I'm going to talk about ...

**含义：** 我要谈论……

**结构：**

I'm going to talk about + 描述对象

**例句：**

I'm going to talk about a person who has had a great influence on me.

我要谈论一位对我影响很大的人。

I'm going to talk about a park that I really enjoy visiting.

我要谈论一个我很喜欢去的公园。

**可替换表达：**

- tell you about：告诉你有关……的事情
    
- describe：描述……
    
- talk about：谈论……
    

## 二、描述事件、经历

用于 Part 2 开头，引出某个时间、事件或经历。

### 1. The time that I'm going to talk about is when ...

**含义：** 我要谈论的那段时间是……

**结构：**

The time that I'm going to talk about is when + 句子

**例句：**

The time that I'm going to talk about is when I traveled to Beijing with my family.

我要谈论的那段时间是我和家人一起去北京旅行的时候。

### 2. I'm going to talk about the time when ...

**含义：** 我要谈论……的时候。

**结构：**

I'm going to talk about the time when + 句子

**例句：**

I'm going to talk about the time when I received an unexpected gift from my best friend.

我要谈论我收到最好的朋友送来的一份意外礼物的那段经历。

## 三、引出或切换话题的句型

这些表达可以帮助你在 Part 2 中自然地引出一个话题，或者从一个方面过渡到另一个方面。

|句型|中文含义|例句|
|---|---|---|
|As for ...|至于……；就……而言|As for its location, the park is quite close to my home.|
|In terms of ...|就……而言|In terms of convenience, smartphones are extremely useful.|
|Speaking of ...|说到……|Speaking of music, I really enjoy listening to pop songs.|
|When it comes to ...|说到……；谈到……|When it comes to food, I prefer homemade meals.|
|As far as ... is concerned|就……而言|As far as technology is concerned, smartphones have revolutionized the way we communicate and access information in today's fast-paced world.|
|Moving on to ...|接下来谈到……|Moving on to its design, I think the building is quite impressive.|

## 四、例句积累

### 1. 描述电影

The movie that I'm going to talk about is a thrilling, suspenseful ride that will keep you on the edge of your seat.

我要谈论的这部电影是一段惊险刺激、扣人心弦的体验，会让你全程屏息以待。

### 2. 描述科技

As far as technology is concerned, smartphones have revolutionized the way we communicate and access information in today's fast-paced world.

就科技而言，智能手机彻底改变了我们在当今快节奏的世界中沟通和获取信息的方式。

## 五、使用建议

1. **开头直接点题：** 可以使用 `I'm going to talk about ...`，简单、清晰，适用于大多数 Part 2 题目。
    
2. **描述具体对象：** 可以使用 `The ... that I'm going to talk about is ...`，例如人物、地点、物品或电影。
    
3. **描述经历：** 可以使用 `I'm going to talk about the time when ...`，自然引出某个具体事件。
    
4. **切换描述角度：** 可以使用 `As for ...`、`In terms of ...` 或 `When it comes to ...`，让回答更有条理。
    

**记忆重点：** 不必背诵所有句型。先熟练掌握 `I'm going to talk about ...`，再根据需要使用其他表达。开头简洁自然，比刻意使用复杂句型更重要。

补充更自然的口语开头区分开头和过渡句用法统一并简化句型分类
