## 填充词和填充句

- um .../well, .../you see .../I mean .../you know ...
- That's a tough/tricky question.
  这是一个棘手的问题。
- I haven't thought about it before, because ...
  我以前没有想过这个问题，因为……
- I don't know much about this, but I guess ...
  我对此了解不多，但我猜……
- Let me see ...
  让我看看……
- Let me think for a second ...
  让我想一想……
- It's hard to say ...
  很难说……
- How can I put it?
  我该怎么说呢?
- Oh, I forget the word. Um ...
  哦，我忘了这个词了。嗯……

## 例句：

- Um ... that's a tough question. I don't think there's a one-size-fits-all solution.
- I mean, I haven't thought about it before, because I've never had to deal with that kind of challenge.
