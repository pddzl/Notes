| 观点类                   | In conclusion, I do believe that ..., while ...<br>总而言之，我认为……尽管……                                                                                                                                 | In conclusion, I do believe that **young criminals** should not receive the same **punishment** as adults, while I also think they should **face appropriate consequences**.<br>总而言之，我认为青少年**罪犯**不应该和成人受到相同的**惩罚**，尽管我认为他们也应该面对**相应的后果**。 |
| 非观点类<br>（原因+措施）       | In conclusion, there are some reasons for the problem as I have mentioned above, and ... can adopt various measures to tackle it.<br>总而言之，这个问题的原因如上文所说，但……可以采取各种措施来应对。                            | In conclusion, there are some reasons for the problem as I have mentioned above, and governments can **adopt various measures** to **tackle** it.<br>总而言之，这个问题的原因如上文所说，政府可以**采取各种措施来应对**。                                                 |
| 非观点类<br>（原因+影响）       | In conclusion, there are some reasons for the problem as I have mentioned above, and ... the positive/negative impacts on ... should also be recognised.<br>总而言之，这个问题的原因如上文所说，它对……积极/消极的影响应该被意识到。 | In conclusion, there are some reasons for the problem as I have mentioned above, and the **negative impacts** on **social resources** should also be recognised.<br>总而言之，这个问题的原因如上文所说，它对**社会资源**的**消极影响**应该被意识到。                          |
