# 回答任务

## 观点类任务

| 观点类任务 | 回答任务的句型                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 同意与否  | - 完全同意：I completely agree with this idea.<br>- 完全不同意：I completely disagree with this idea.<br>- 部分同意：I partly agree with this idea.                                                                                             |
| 双边讨论  | - 赞同前者：I agree with the former opinion.<br>- 赞同后者：I agree with the latter opinion.<br>- 有自己的观点：I do believe that ...                                                                                                            |
| 利弊比较  | - 利大于弊：I do believe that the benefits may outweigh the drawbacks.<br>- 弊大于利：I do believe that the drawbacks may outweigh the benefits.<br>- 利弊平衡：I do believe that the benefits and drawbacks of the issue are evenly balanced. |
| 积极消极  | - 积极：I do believe that it is generally a positive development.<br>- 消极：I do believe that it is generally a negative development.<br>- 既有积极又有消极：This development has both positive and negative aspects.                         |

  

## 非观点类任务

| 非观点类任务 | 回答任务的句型                                                                                       |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 原因+措施  | Several factors may contribute to the issue, and some measures should be taken to address it. |
| 原因+影响  | Several factors may contribute to the issue, and it probably leads to some influences on ...  |

  

## 混合类任务

将观点类和非观点类的句型进行组合，灵活应对即可。

例如：【原因】Several factors may contribute to the issue and【积极消极】I do believe that it is generally a positive/negative development.

| 任务类型                                | 点明主题的句型                                                                                                  | 完整例句                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 同意与否                                | It is sometimes argued that ...<br>有人认为……                                                                | It is sometimes argued that **juveniles involved in illegal activities** should face the same **consequences** as adults.<br>有人认为，参与**非法活动**的青少年应该面对与成年人同样的**后果**。                                                                                                               |
| 双边讨论                                | Some people argue/claim/content that ..., while others argue/claim/content that ...<br>一些人认为……，而其他人则认为…… | Some people contend that **constructing** an underground railway system can **reduce the stress on** transport, while others claim that expanding roads can achieve this goal.<br>有些人认为**建设**地铁系统可以**减轻交通的压力**，而另一些人则认为扩建道路可以达到这个目的。                                             |
