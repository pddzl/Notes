## 开头段

  

- **开头段基本结构**：点明主题 + 回答任务

- **注意事项**：

- 时间控制：3 分钟内完成

- 长度建议：2 句话

- **写作重点**：清楚地说明主题，准确地回答任务，尽量不出错

  

## ① 开头段的写法 - 点明主题

  

- **同意与否类**：It is sometimes argued that ... 有人认为……

- **双边讨论类**：Some people argue/claim/content that ..., while others argue/claim/content that ... 一些人认为……，而其他人则认为……

- **积极消极类、利弊比较类、非观点类（原因+措施、原因+影响）**：It is observed that ... 据观察……

  

> 注意：面对例外情况，灵活选择其他合适的句型

  

## ② 开头段的写法 - 回答任务

  

### 观点类

  

**同意与否**

  

- 完全同意：I completely agree with this idea.

- 完全不同意：I completely disagree with this idea.

- 部分同意：I partly agree with this idea.

  

**双边讨论**

  

- 赞同前者：I agree with the former opinion.

- 赞同后者：I agree with the latter opinion.

- 有自己的观点：I do believe that ...

  

**利弊比较**

  

- 利大于弊：I do believe that the benefits may outweigh the drawbacks.

- 弊大于利：I do believe that the drawbacks may outweigh the benefits.

- 利弊平衡：I do believe that the benefits and drawbacks of the issue are evenly balanced.

  

**积极消极**

  

- 积极：I do believe that it is generally a positive development.

- 消极：I do believe that it is generally a negative development.

- 既有积极又有消极：This development has both positive and negative aspects.

  

### 非观点类

  

- **原因+措施**：Several factors may contribute to the issue, and some measures should be taken to address it.

- **原因+影响**：Several factors may contribute to the issue, and it probably leads to some influences on ...

  

### 混合类

  

- 结合观点类和非观点类，灵活应对即可
