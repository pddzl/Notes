- ﻿﻿Overall, it is clear that...
- ﻿﻿Overall, it can be clearly seen that...
- ﻿﻿Overall, it can be seen from the 2 that...

• Overall, it can be clearly seen that the sales of mobile phones increased significantly from 2010 to 2015.
