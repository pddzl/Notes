## 1. 地图题的分类、结构和时态

### 1.1 地图题分类

1. **历史变迁题（Changes over time）**
    
    - 展现同一地点在不同时间点的变迁或者变化
    - 例如：比较某个城镇在 1990 年和 2020 年的布局

2. **规划方案题（Proposed changes / Future plans）**
    
    - 展示某个地点未来的规划或改造方案
    - 有时会给出两个不同的选址，要求分析每个方案的优劣并得出结论（较少见）

### 1.2 地图题结构

和其他 Task 1 图表相同，包括三个部分：

1. **Introduction（开头段）**：概括地图展示的地点和时间
2. **Overview（概述段）**：总结地图最明显的整体变化或主要特征
3. **Body Paragraphs（主体段）**：具体描述地图中的位置、布局和变化

地图题通常有两张地图（Before and After），主体段一般可以分为两段

- Body 1：描述原始地图（Before）的布局
- Body 2：描述后续地图（After）的变化

注意：不一定必须一张地图对应一段，也可以按照区域或变化类型分组

### 1.3 地图题时态

|地图时间|使用时态|
|---|---|
|过去与过去|一般过去时|
|过去与现在|一般过去时 + 一般现在时|
|现在与未来|一般现在时 + 一般将来时|

注意：

- 描述过去发生的变化：一般过去时
- 描述现在的布局：一般现在时
- 描述未来规划：一般将来时

## 2. 地图开头段（Introduction）

### 2.1 写作要求

- 用一句话概括图表的内容
- 介绍地图展示的地点和时间
- 改写图表名称时，建议统一使用 `map / maps`

### 2.2 常用句型

**句型 1：The map(s) show(s) ...**

The maps show the changes that took place in a town between 1990 and 2020.

**句型 2：The map(s) illustrate(s) ...**

The maps illustrate how a town changed over a period of 30 years.

**句型 3：The map(s) compare(s) ...**

The maps compare the layout of a village in 1990 with its current layout.

## 3. 概述段（Overview）

### 3.1 概述内容

地图题的 Overview 主要概括最明显的整体特征，不需要描述所有细节

可以关注：

- **整体变化程度**：变化明显，还是基本保持不变
- **主要新增或消失的设施**：是否增加或拆除了建筑、道路等
- **土地用途变化**：例如农田变成住宅区、工业区变成休闲区

### 3.2 常用表达

- Overall, the area underwent significant changes over the period.
- Overall, the layout of the area remained largely unchanged.
- Overall, the most noticeable change was the construction of a new residential area.
- Overall, the area became more developed, with several new facilities being added.

## 4. 主体段（Body Paragraphs）

### 4.1 描述顺序

描述地图时，可以选择固定的空间顺序：

- 从左到右：from west to east
- 从上到下：from north to south
- 顺时针：in a clockwise direction
- 逆时针：in an anticlockwise direction

建议选择一种顺序，并尽量保持一致。

### 4.2 描述位置

**句型 1：描述对象 + 表示位置的动词 + 方位 + 参照物**

- A cafe stands to the northwest of the square.
- The school is located to the southwest of the housing area.
- The railway station is situated in the centre of the town.

**句型 2：There be + 描述对象 + 方位 + 参照物**

- There is a cafe to the northwest of the square.
- There was a school in the north of the town.

**表示位置的常用动词：**

|表达|语态|含义|
|---|---|---|
|be|主动结构|位于|
|stand|主动语态|坐落于|
|be located|被动结构|位于|
|be situated|被动结构|坐落于|

注意：

- `stand`、`lie`、`sit` 可以使用主动语态描述位置
- `be located`、`be situated` 使用被动结构

### 4.3 方位词

|方位|英文|
|---|---|
|北方|north|
|南方|south|
|东方|east|
|西方|west|
|东北方|northeast|
|东南方|southeast|
|西北方|northwest|
|西南方|southwest|

### 4.4 方位介词和位置表达

| 中文含义             | 英文表达                            | 例子                               |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 在某个区域内部的东/西/南/北方 | in the east/west/south/north of | in the north of the town         |
| 在某个地点外部的东/西/南/北方 | to the east/west/south/north of | to the east of the town          |
| 靠近               | near                            | near the station                 |
| 紧挨着、毗邻           | next to                         | next to the school               |
| 在某个区域的中心         | in the centre of                | in the centre of the village     |
| 在...之间           | between A and B                 | between the school and the park  |
| 沿着               | along                           | along the main road              |
| 越过、在...上方        | over                            | over the river                   |
| 在...的对面          | across from                     | across from the school           |
| 在...的交叉口         | at the intersection of          | at the intersection of two roads |
| 在...旁边           | beside                          | beside the railway station       |
| 在...后面           | behind                          | behind the school                |
| 在...前面           | in front of                     | in front of the house            |

**重点区别：**

- `in the north of`：在某个区域内部的北部
- `to the north of`：在某个区域外部的北方
- `at the top/bottom of the map`：在地图的顶部/底部

## 5. 地图变化（Changes）

地图题常见的变化类型及表达如下：

| 含义     | 动词原形 / 表达        | 被动语态              |
| ------ | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| 新增     | add              | be added          |
| 建造     | build            | be built          |
| 建造     | construct        | be constructed    |
| 扩大     | expand           | be expanded       |
| 扩大     | enlarge          | be enlarged       |
| 延伸、扩建  | extend           | be extended       |
| 缩小、减少  | reduce           | be reduced        |
| 移除     | remove           | be removed        |
| 取代、替代  | replace          | be replaced by    |
| 让位于... | make way for     | —                 |
| 搬迁、迁移  | relocate         | be relocated      |
| 保持不变   | remain unchanged | —                 |
| 拆除建筑物  | demolish         | be demolished     |
| 改造成... | convert into     | be converted into |

### 5.1 新增（Addition）

- A new school was built in the north of the town.
- A supermarket was constructed next to the railway station.
- A new car park was added to the east of the village.

常用名词表达：

- an addition to：新增的部分
- the construction of：...的建设
- the introduction of：...的引入

### 5.2 扩大或延伸（Expansion）

- The residential area was expanded.
- The school was enlarged.
- The road was extended to the north.

注意：

- `expand / enlarge`：强调面积或规模扩大
- `extend`：强调向某个方向延伸或扩建

### 5.3 缩小（Reduction）

- The size of the park was reduced.
- The forest area shrank considerably.
- The farmland decreased in size.

### 5.4 移除或拆除（Removal）

- The old factory was demolished.
- Several houses were removed.
- The farmland was cleared.

### 5.5 替换或改造（Replacement / Conversion）

- The old factory was replaced by a shopping centre.
- The farmland was converted into a residential area.
- The old railway station was turned into a museum.

注意区别：

- `A was replaced by B`：A 被 B 替换
- `A was converted into B`：A 被改造成 B
- `B made way for A`：B 为 A 让出了位置

### 5.6 迁移（Relocation）

- The school was relocated to the north of the town.
- The car park was moved to the eastern side of the road.

### 5.7 保持不变（No change）

- The railway station remained unchanged.
- The main road remained in the same location.
- The river remained in its original position.

## 6. 描述位置或变化的进阶句型

### 6.1 描述新设施出现在原有设施的位置

**句型：**

There be + 新的描述对象 + 方位 + 参照物 + where + 原有描述对象 + used to be.

`There will be a third school building to the northeast of the second school building where a sports field used to be.`

含义：原来是运动场的位置，将新建第三栋教学楼，位于第二栋教学楼的东北方

注意：`used to be` 表示某个地方过去存在某物，但现在已经不存在

### 6.2 描述原有设施的位置及其变化

**句型：**

描述对象 + which + 表示位置的动词 + 原来的方位和参照物 + 表示变化的动词 + 具体变化内容.

`The path, which was situated between the first and second school buildings, will be reduced in length to allow the two buildings to be connected.`

含义：原来位于第一栋和第二栋教学楼之间的小路将缩短，以便连接两栋建筑

常用变化表达：

- make way for：为...让出位置
- remain unchanged：保持不变
- be expanded：被扩大
- be extended：被延伸
- be reduced：被缩小
- be removed：被移除
- be replaced：被替换
- be relocated：被迁移

### 6.3 描述位置的基础句型

- A cafe stands to the northwest of the square.
- There is a cafe to the northwest of the square.

这两种表达都可以用于描述地图中的位置

## 7. 地图题写作流程

1. 审题
    
    - 确定地图展示的地点和时间
    - 判断是历史变迁还是未来规划
    - 确定使用的时态

2. 观察整体变化
    
    - 找出新增、拆除、扩建、缩小和迁移的设施
    - 找出保持不变的部分
    - 判断整体变化程度

3. 确定 Overview
    
    - 概括最明显的整体变化
    - 不需要罗列所有具体细节

4. **划分主体段**
    
    - 按照地图区域或变化类型分组
    - 确定统一的描述顺序

5. **描述具体内容**
    
    - 先描述位置，再描述变化
    - 使用准确的方位介词和变化动词
    - 注意时态和语态

6. **检查**
    
    - 方位是否准确？
    - 时态是否正确？
    - 是否遗漏重要变化？
    - 是否存在重复描述？
