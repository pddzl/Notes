描述地图中的位置：

- ﻿句型1：描述对象＋表示 “位于，坐落”的动词（主动/被动语态）+方位+参照物
- ﻿﻿句型2:There be +描述对象+方位+ 参照物
- ﻿表示“位于，坐落”的动词：
	- ﻿be动词/stand（用主动语态）
	- ﻿﻿be located/be situated (用被动语态)

描述地图中的位置或事物变化：

- ﻿句型1：There be+新的描述对象+方位词+参照物+ where +同一位置上旧的描述对象 + used to be
- ﻿句型2：描述对象，which+表示“位于，坐落”的动词（主动/被动语态）+旧的方位+旧的参照物，表示变化的动词+ 具体的变化内容
	﻿表示变化的动词：
		﻿﻿make way for/remain unchanged (用主动语态)
		be expanded/extended/removed/replaced/relocated (用被态语态）

例句：

- ﻿﻿A café stands to the northwest of the square.
- ﻿﻿There is a café to the northwest of the square.
- ﻿﻿There will be a third school building to the northeast of the second school building where a sports field used to be.
- ﻿﻿The path, which was situated between the first and second school buildings, will be reduced in length to allow the two buildings to be connected.
