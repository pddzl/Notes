## 1. 表达比较（Comparison）

### 1.1. 表达"比...更多 / 更少"

| 表达                     | 用法      | 例句                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| more + 可数/不可数名词 + than | 比……更多   | The UK consumed **more energy than** France. |
| fewer + 可数名词复数 + than  | 比……数量更少 | Japan had **fewer visitors than** China.     |
| less + 不可数名词 + than    | 比……更少   | France used **less water than** Germany.     |

**注意：**
- **fewer** 用于可数名词复数。
- **less** 用于不可数名词。

### 1.2. 表达"和...一样多"

|表达|用法|例句|
|---|---|---|
|as many + 可数名词复数 + as|和……数量一样多|France had **as many students as** Germany.|
|as much + 不可数名词 + as|和……数量一样多|Canada consumed **as much oil as** Australia.|

### 1.3. 表达"是...的几倍 / 几分之几"

**结构：**

倍数词 / 分数词 + as many / much + 名词 + as

- The UK had **twice as many visitors as** France.
    
- Germany consumed **three times as much energy as** Italy.
    
- The figure for Japan was **half as high as** that for China.

**注意：**

- 可数名词复数使用 **as many**。
    
- 不可数名词使用 **as much**。
    
- 描述比例、数值等时，也可以使用 **twice as high as**、**half as high as** 等结构。

### 1.4. 用副词描述数量差异的程度

| 副词            | 中文含义 | 例句                                                                               |
| ------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| slightly      | 略微地  | The figure for France was **slightly higher than** that for Germany.             |
| significantly | 显著地  | The number of visitors to China was **significantly higher than** that to Japan. |

## 2. 表达最值（Superlatives）

### 2.1. 表达“最多的”

| 表达                             | 用法       | 例句                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| the most + 名词                  | 最多的...   | China had **the most visitors**.                               |
| the highest number of + 可数名词复数 | ...的数量最高 | China had **the highest number of visitors**.                  |
| the highest amount of + 不可数名词  | ...的总量最高 | Germany recorded **the highest amount of energy consumption**. |
| the highest proportion of + 名词 | ...的占比最高 | Japan had **the highest proportion of elderly people**.        |

### 2. 表达“最少的”

| 表达                            | 用法       | 例句                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| the fewest + 可数名词复数           | 数量最少的    | Italy had **the fewest visitors**.                           |
| the least + 不可数名词             | 数量最少的    | France consumed **the least energy**.                        |
| the lowest number of + 可数名词复数 | ...的数量最低 | Italy had **the lowest number of visitors**.                 |
| the lowest amount of + 不可数名词  | ...的总量最低 | France recorded **the lowest amount of energy consumption**. |
| the lowest proportion of + 名词 | ...的占比最低 | Germany had **the lowest proportion of elderly people**.     |

**注意：**

- **most / fewest** 常用于直接修饰名词。
    
- **highest / lowest number** 常用于描述可数事物的数量。
    
- **highest / lowest amount** 常用于描述不可数事物的数量或总量。
    
- **highest / lowest proportion** 用于描述占比。

## 3. 表达"大多数"（The Majority）

### 3.1. the majority of + 可数名词复数

通常搭配复数动词。

**例句：**

The majority of students **were** aged between 18 and 25.

大多数学生的年龄在 18 至 25 岁之间。

### 3.2. the majority of + 不可数名词

通常搭配单数动词。

**例句：**

The majority of the water **was** used for agriculture.

大部分水被用于农业。

**注意：** 谓语动词通常根据 `of` 后面的名词来判断单复数。

## 4. 描述百分比（Percentages）

### 4.1. 方式一：直接写百分比

直接使用数字和百分号。

**例句：**

The proportion of students who chose science was **20%**.

### 4.2. 方式二：将百分比转换为分数

例如：20% = one fifth；约 66.7% = two thirds。

|中文|英文表达|百分比|
|---|---|---|
|四分之一|a/one quarter|25%|
|三分之一|a/one third|约 33.3%|
|二分之一|a/one half / half|50%|
|三分之二|two thirds|约 66.7%|
|四分之三|three quarters|75%|
|五分之一|a/one fifth|20%|
|五分之二|two fifths|40%|

**构成规则：**

- **几分之一：** a/one + 序数词，如 `one fifth`。
    
- **几分之几：** 基数词 + 序数词复数形式，如 `two thirds`、`three quarters`。

### 4.3. 方式三：使用常见分数表达

对于 25%、50%、75%，可以分别使用：

- **25%：** a/one quarter
    
- **50%：** a/one half / half
    
- **75%：** three quarters

**例句：**

One quarter of the population lived in rural areas.

四分之一的人口居住在农村地区。

Half of the students chose science.

一半的学生选择了科学。

Three quarters of the respondents preferred online shopping.

四分之三的受访者更喜欢网上购物。

## 5. 提升数据描述准确性

### 1. 接近...，略低于...

**表达：** just under / nearly / almost + 数值

**例句：**

The figure stood at **just under 50%**.

该数值略低于 50%。

The proportion was **nearly 60%**.

该比例接近 60%。

### 4.2. 刚刚超过...，略高于...

**表达：** just over + 数值

**例句：**

The figure was **just over 30%**.

该数值略高于 30%。

### 4.3. 大约是...

**表达：** around / about / roughly / approximately + 数值

**例句：**

The proportion was **approximately 40%**.

该比例约为 40%。

The figure stood at **around 200,000**.

该数值约为 20 万。

## 6. 表达"占比多少"的动词（词组）

### 6.1. account for

**含义：** 占……比例

**例句：**

Renewable energy **accounted for 25% of** total energy consumption.

可再生能源占总能源消耗的 25%。

### 6.2. make up

**含义：** 占……比例；构成……

**例句：**

Women **made up 60% of** the workforce.

女性占劳动力的 60%。

### 6.3. constitute

**含义：** 构成；占……

**例句：**

Online sales **constituted 40% of** total sales.

线上销售额占总销售额的 40%。

**注意：** 描述过去图表中的数据时，通常使用过去式，例如 `accounted for`、`made up`、`constituted`。
