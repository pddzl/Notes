图表特征-趋势

·上升趋势:increase, see/show/experience an upward trend
·下降趋势:decrease, see/show/experience a downward trend
·几乎不变:remain stable, experience little change

图表特征-趋势程度
·猛烈:dramatically/notably, dramatic/notable
轻微:slightly/moderately, slight/moderate

例句
· The average carbon dioxide emission in the UK has seen a downward
trend.

## 表达比较

  

- 比……更多：more + 名词 + than ...

- 比……更少：fewer + 可数名词 + than ... / less + 不可数名词 + than ...

- 和……一样多：as many + 可数名词 + as ... / as much + 不可数名词 + as ...

- 和……的几倍/几分之几一样多：倍数词/分数词 + as many/much + 名词 + as ...

  

## 用副词进一步描述数量差异上的程度大小

  

- 轻微地：slightly

- 显著地：significantly

  

## 表达最值

  

- 最多的：the most + 名词

- 最高数量/比例的：the highest number/amount/proportion of + 名词

- 最少的：the fewest + 可数名词 / the least + 不可数名词

- 最低数量/比例的：the lowest number/amount/proportion of + 名词

  

## 表达"大多数的"

  

- the majority of + 可数名词复数（+ 动词复数形式）

- the majority of + 不可数名词（+ 动词单数形式）
